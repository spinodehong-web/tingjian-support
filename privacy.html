Privacy Policy · 隐私政策

Tingjian - Global Radio

Effective Date: November 24, 2026 · Applies to Tingjian - Global Radio (iOS) and this support site.

1. Data We Collect / 我们收集的数据

We do not collect personal data. Tingjian does not require an account, does not include advertisements, does not integrate third-party analytics or crash-tracking SDKs, and does not track you across apps or websites.

我们不收集任何个人数据。本应用无需注册或登录，不含广告、第三方统计或追踪 SDK，不会跨应用或跨网站追踪您。

2. On-Device Storage / 设备本地存储

Tingjian stores only your playback volume preference locally on your device via iOS UserDefaults (key tingjian.ios.volume). This setting never leaves your device and is removed when you delete the app.

应用仅通过系统本地 UserDefaults 保存音量偏好设置，不会上传至开发者或任何服务器；卸载应用即可清除。

3. Third-Party Network Connections / 第三方网络连接

To display station lists and play audio, your device connects directly to external public services:

  • Radio Browser API (*.api.radio-browser.info): queried for public station metadata and playback click counters.
  • Internet Archive (archive.org): queried for Creative Commons and public-domain lecture audio.
  • Independent Radio Stream Hosts: when you play a station or load its station icon, your device connects directly to that broadcaster's server.

As with any direct internet connection, those third-party servers automatically receive standard network information (such as your IP address and user-agent string) subject to their own privacy policies. The developer does not operate those servers and does not receive their logs.

4. Permissions / 系统权限

Tingjian does not request access to your location, microphone, camera, photos, contacts, or tracking permissions.

5. Contact & Updates / 联系与更新

Questions about privacy can be submitted via our public Support Issues page (please do not post sensitive personal details publicly). If the app's data practices change in future versions, this page and its effective date will be updated accordingly.
