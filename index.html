Tingjian - Global Radio

Lightweight iOS player for live internet radio stations, classical symphony streams, and public-domain talks.

Overview & Features / 功能说明

  • Three Channels: Browse All Stations, Symphony & Classical, and Talks & Lectures.
  • Search & Filters: Filter by language, region, or genre, search stations, and return to your previous list position.
  • Background Playback: Continues playing with iOS Lock Screen and Control Center controls.
  • No Account Needed: Open and listen right away—no sign-in, no ads, and no tracking.

Audio Sources / 音源说明

Tingjian connects directly from your device to public third-party audio streams discovered via the community-maintained Radio Browser directory, and Creative Commons or public-domain lecture audio from Internet Archive. Tingjian does not host streams, record audio, or download audio files to your device.

Because live streams are operated by independent broadcasters, individual stations may occasionally go offline or vary in availability by region.

Troubleshooting / 常见问题

  • A station won't start: Live broadcaster links can be temporarily unavailable. Try another station or check your network connection.
  • No progress bar on live radio: Live radio streams do not have a fixed duration; seeking is available only for on-demand lecture recordings with known length.
  • Interface language: In version 0.3.0, in-app navigation labels are in Chinese while station titles reflect global directory listings.
